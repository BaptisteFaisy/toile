Codelab

“Giving a QNX VM a display” — four chapters, from the problem to a working desktop in a window. Chapters 1–2 run on any Linux machine; chapters 3–4 depend on the validated target. Full text in codelab/.

1 · A display for a QNX VM

Why Screen is unusable in a VM without passthrough, what virtio-gpu is, and the two sides (QEMU host first). Boot a VM with -device virtio-gpu-pci and see nothing appear without the driver.

runs anywhere

2 · The portable core

make build, write your first fixture in the DSL, read the verdicts, the three output formats, the state hash and the C↔Python differential. 100% executable on an ordinary machine.

runs anywhere

3 · The guest driver

PCI enumeration, transport reuse from Passerelle, the controlq/cursorq, the /dev/gpu0 resource manager, building with qcc, and trial E1 as the check.

target dependent

4 · Screen and the demo

The Screen display driver interface, one framebuffer and one head, the mkifs image, the QEMU scripts, and the <3 min demo for the submission.

target dependent
